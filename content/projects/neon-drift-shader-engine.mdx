## Overview

Neon Drift explores arcade racing feel through custom vertex displacement shaders and dynamic track audio-reactivity.

## Technical Highlights

- **Custom Shader Graph Pipeline**: Multi-pass screen space illumination and velocity-weighted blur shaders.
- **Raycast Suspension Physics**: Deterministic vehicle suspension simulation providing responsive arcade drift mechanics.
